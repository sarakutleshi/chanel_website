The Craft

Savoir-Faire

Made by Hand, Born of Tradition

Every CHANEL jewelry piece is born in the ateliers of Paris, where a community of artisans — jewelers, setters, polishers — carry forward centuries of French craft tradition.

The process begins with a sketch. From concept to creation, each piece passes through dozens of pairs of skilled hands, each contributing a layer of precision and care that cannot be replicated by machine.

This commitment to handcraft is what makes every CHANEL jewel not just an accessory, but an object of lasting significance — a piece to be worn, admired, and passed down.

Care Guide

◇

Storage

Store each piece individually in its original pouch or a soft-lined box to prevent scratching. Keep away from direct sunlight and humidity.

◇

Cleaning

Gently wipe gold and platinum pieces with a soft, lint-free cloth. For diamonds, use a mild soap solution and rinse thoroughly with warm water.

◇

Wearing

Remove jewelry before exercising, swimming or applying perfume and cosmetics. Put on your jewelry as the last step of your getting ready routine.

◇

Servicing

We recommend bringing your CHANEL jewelry and watches to a boutique for professional cleaning and inspection every two years.
