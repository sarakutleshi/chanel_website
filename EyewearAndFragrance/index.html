The Design

Design Philosophy

Where Vision Meets Elegance

Every CHANEL eyewear frame is designed in Paris, where the House's artistic codes — the camellia, the quilted pattern, the interlocked CC — are reinterpreted in acetate, metal and crystal.

The result is eyewear that is unmistakably CHANEL: refined yet playful, structured yet graceful — frames that frame not just the eyes, but the entire face with intention.

Each pair is crafted from the finest materials, with lenses that meet the most rigorous optical standards, ensuring that style and protection are never a compromise.

The Olfactory World

Top Notes

The Opening

The first impression — bright, volatile accords of citrus, bergamot, and aldehydes that announce a fragrance's character within seconds of application.

Grapefruit · Bergamot · Green leaves · Aldehydes
Heart Notes

The Soul

The signature of a fragrance — floral, spicy or woody accords that emerge as the top notes fade, lasting several hours on the skin.

Jasmine · Rose · Ylang-Ylang · Iris
Base Notes

The Memory

The lasting impression — rich, deep accords of musk, amber and wood that anchor the fragrance and linger on skin and fabric for hours.

Patchouli · Sandalwood · Vetiver · Musk
